## Important technical distinctions

### Spin coating vs. sputtering

Both can produce thin films, but **spin coating** distributes a liquid over a rotating substrate, whereas **sputtering** deposits material ejected from a target in a vacuum/plasma process. They are not interchangeable for all materials or desired film properties.

### Lithography vs. etching

**Lithography** produces the patterned mask (often in photoresist). **Etching** transfers a selected pattern by removing exposed material. A maskless aligner is not itself a dry etcher.

### Etching vs. dicing

**Dry etching** locally removes material in selected regions of a device. **Dicing** physically separates a substrate or wafer into individual pieces, commonly near the end of processing.

### Laser cutting vs. laser micro-soldering

The **ULS VLS 3.50** is a laser cutting/engraving platform; a **laser micro-soldering station** locally heats solder for joining. The equipment described in the provided report should not be presented as the same machine.
