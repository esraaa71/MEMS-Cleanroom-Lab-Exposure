# Laurell WS-650MZ-23NPPB — Spin Coater

**Process area:** Liquid-film application

## Principle and role

Spin coating spreads a dispensed liquid across a rotating substrate. Spin speed, acceleration, viscosity, and time affect resulting film thickness and uniformity. Photoresist coating is a common use before lithography.
