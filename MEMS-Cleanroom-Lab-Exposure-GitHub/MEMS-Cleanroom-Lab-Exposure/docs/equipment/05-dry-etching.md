# Dry Etching — Model Not Identified

**Process area:** Material removal / pattern transfer

## Principle and role

Dry etching removes material from selected wafer regions; many implementations use plasma-generated ions/radicals. Reactive ion etching (RIE) and inductively coupled plasma (ICP) are **examples**, but the specific instrument type in this lab was not provided.

## Evidence and limits

Only a dry-etching machine was mentioned in the user-supplied equipment list; no model, photo, operating mode, chemistry, or results were supplied. Do not label it RIE or ICP without confirmation.
