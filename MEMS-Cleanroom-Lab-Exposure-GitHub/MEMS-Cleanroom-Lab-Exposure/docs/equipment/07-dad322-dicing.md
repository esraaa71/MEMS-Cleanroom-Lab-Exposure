# DISCO DAD322 — Automatic Dicing Saw

**Process area:** Wafer/substrate singulation

## Principle and role

A precision dicing saw mechanically cuts processed wafers or other substrates into individual die or sample pieces. It serves a different purpose from maskless lithographic patterning.

## Evidence and limits

The four-page MEMS lab summary describes and photographs a DISCO DAD322. The repository does not include independent dicing results.
