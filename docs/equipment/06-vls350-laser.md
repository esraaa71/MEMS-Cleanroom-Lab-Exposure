# Universal Laser Systems VLS 3.50 — Laser Cutter/Engraver

**Process area:** Laser cutting and engraving

## Principle and role

The VLS 3.50 is a laser platform used for cutting and engraving compatible materials; the exact capabilities depend on its installed laser and optics. It is **not** equivalent to a laser micro-soldering station.
